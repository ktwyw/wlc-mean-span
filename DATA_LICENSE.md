## Third-party material

- The Milne extrapolation length of the angular-diffusion transport problem,
  `z_M = 0.71821164`, is a published result of E. Amic, J. M. Luck and
  Th. M. Nieuwenhuizen, J. Phys. A: Math. Gen. 29, 4915 (1996). It is used as an
  input constant; it is not a result of this work.
- The first-return kernel of the random-acceleration process used in the
  short-chain analysis is due to H. P. McKean (1963) and T. W. Burkhardt (2014);
  see the article's reference list.
