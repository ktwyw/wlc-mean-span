# Release validation (2026-11-11, release v1.2.0)

- **Manuscript and supplement.** "Mean span dimension of an ideal wormlike chain". The manuscript sources and
  PDFs are not part of the public repository. Both documents are compiled from a clean temporary tree with
  REVTeX 4.2 and the AIP numeric bibliography style (fixed `SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH`, byte-identical on recompilation).
  - The article has 21 preprint pages, 4 figures and 1 table; the abstract has 247 words.
  - The supplement has 15 pages, 1 figure, 1 table and one algorithm. Every reference it cites is also cited in
    the article.
  - There are no appendices. The two documents do not read each other's labels, so each compiles on its own.
    A small set of cross-references is written as plain text: the article cites the supplement's five
    top-level sections, its figure, table and algorithm; the supplement names the article's main results and
    gives their equation numbers at first mention. The compile script checks these numbers against both
    documents and fails if either numbering changes.
  - The repository files refer to the article's results by name. Equation numbers appear in one place only,
    the index in `tutorial/README.md`; figure numbers are used throughout.
  - There are no undefined or multiply defined references or citations in either document (checked by the
    compile script), and no overfull lines. No placeholders remain.
- **Reference table.** `data/wlc_mean_span_reference.csv` (67 nodes), unchanged since the second review round
  (`code/build_reference_curve.py`: ordered Schur, alias-corrected inversion with A = 12, check order 128);
  rerunning a node reproduces all columns. Earlier versions are compared in `reference_table_versions.csv`;
  the inversion-noise audit for λ ≥ 100 is in `inversion_noise_audit.csv`.
- **Checks added in rounds three and four.** `telegraph_inversion_test.csv` (`code/telegraph_inversion_test.py`):
  the production inversion of the exact telegraph transform at all 67 lengths has errors −1.5e-10 to −2.9e-11,
  equal to the predicted remaining alias to 1e-14. `data/long_chain_remainder_check.*`: the table agrees with
  the long-chain expansion plus a remainder c2 λ^-3/2 + c4 λ^-5/2 (fitted on 100 ≤ λ ≤ 1500; the same on 100–700) to 2.7e-9
  for 10³ ≤ λ ≤ 2×10⁴; for λ ≥ 3×10⁴ the residuals are 0.2–1.0 ε_int and the inversion parameter A = 10, 14
  moves F_∞ by up to 1.4 ε_int.
- **Checks added after the readiness review.** Heat-kernel path sampling at L/p = 10³ and 10⁴
  (`data/heat_path_long.csv`): 39.798 ± 0.035 and 129.09 ± 0.16 against the table's 39.787 and 128.86
  (supplementary material, Fig. S1).
- **Two runs added before submission (2026-11-11).** Three more batches of 20 000 chains at L/p = 10⁴
  (`data/heat_path_long_extra.csv`); the pooled 80 000-chain estimate is 128.890 ± 0.079 against 128.864
  (`data/heat_path_long_pooled.csv`), so all six lengths now agree within one standard error. A separable
  finite-volume extrapolation at L/p = 100 (`data/finite_volume_long_summary.json`) agrees with the spectral value to
  −3.1×10⁻⁵ (observed order) and −2.1×10⁻⁵ (integer order).
- **Audits on the final table.** Conditioning at every production transform point
  (`data/conditioning_audit_full.csv`, max 1.4602); eigendecomposition vs Schur (`data/solver_regression_audit.csv`,
  ≤ 5.8e-12); orders 128–256 (`data/high_order_spot_checks.csv`); finite-volume convergence orders and
  extrapolation-model sensitivity (`data/finite_volume_order_analysis.csv`); formula error by node set with the
  printed coefficients (`data/rational_fit_metrics.json`). Comparisons with published simulations were removed
  from the paper and the public package.
- **Figures.** Regenerated by `code/plot_main_figures.py` from archived data: Figs. 1–4 and supplementary
  Fig. S1. The PDFs carry no creation date and regenerate byte-identically. Each figure was inspected at
  final size.
  - All figures pass the 5 pt glyph audit.
  - The PDF collision audit passes for Figs. 2, 4 and S1; in Fig. 3 it flags only the radical bar of √π in the panel (d) axis label (visual
    check: no overlap). In Fig. 1 it reports only the oversized bounding
    boxes of the ⟨ ⟩ and bracket delimiter glyphs; a visual check shows no overlap.
- **Derivation check (2026-11-11).** Every derivation in the article and the supplement was re-derived by hand
  and with SymPy; no error was found. The reciprocity value of the second-order matching constant a₂ and the
  three moment identities behind B were tested against a direct solution of the second-order boundary problem
  (`code/check_second_order_matching.py`, `data/second_order_matching_check.csv`): agreement to 1e-13 at
  angular orders 32, 64 and 128. Tutorial chapters 2 to 6 now give each derivation step with a symbolic or
  numerical check, and `tutorial/README.md` indexes every numbered equation.
- **Scope of the public package.** Material belonging to results that are not in the article (rigorous
  bounds, a cubic short-chain coefficient) and the figures of an earlier, longer supplement were removed
  before the first public release. The exact end-to-end moments moved to `code/wlc_moments.py`; the column
  `rigorous_moment_lower` was dropped from the three `data/heat_path_*.csv` tables (all other fields
  byte-identical). After the change, the endpoint analysis, the tables, Figs. 1–4 and Fig. S1 regenerate
  byte-identically, and all notebooks execute.
- **Notebooks and tutorial.** `scripts/build_notebooks.py` and `scripts/build_tutorial.py` executed the
  4 figure notebooks and the 10 tutorial notebooks (an overview and nine chapters) without error; chapter 9
  passes 117 of 117 checks, which cover most of the numbers revised in the last three review rounds (the rest
  are traceable to the files named in the supplementary material and `data/README.md`).
